No in-game screenshots yet

And we are not going to draw them on the owner's behalf. A screenshot from someone else's mod, or a picture put there “to look nice”, is worse than empty space: people install the mod expecting what they saw.

What needs to be captured for this section to come alive:

  • Before-and-after pairs — the same view in the base game and in Nova. Most valuable where the difference is visible at a glance: a tile with a new luxury, the policy screen with the new branches, the era-bonus choice.
  • Short clips of mechanics — 3 to 8 seconds, no sound. For example: choosing an era bonus when entering an era, the in-game voice commands on the H key, switching a unit skin in the launcher — the last two come with the “Gold” subscription.
  • One large shot for the header — what a first-time visitor sees when the site opens.

Files go into assets/img/, captions into data/site.json, section gallery. The format of each entry is described inside that file. No HTML editing needed.
