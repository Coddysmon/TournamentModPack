The short version

TopicWhat's there
CivilizationsAn era bonus for each one: entering an era you choose from four options instead of three, and the fourth is yours alone. 43 national bonuses and 21 shared ones.
HeritageA new policy branch where the era bonuses live: 64 policies.
PathsA new branch of seven policies about roads, caravans and keeping the empire connected. Available from turn one — no era requirement.
BulwarkA new defensive branch, unlocked in the Medieval era. Walls, castle, arsenal and military base stop being a pure drain on gold.
Religion58 new beliefs, including pantheons tied to resources.
Resources15 new luxuries — each with its own model on the tile.
Buildings41 new buildings, some of them fed by the city's luxuries.
WondersNew wonders with effects and icons of their own.
UnitsNine new units, including unique ones for civilizations.
TilesNew improvements: an admiralty from a Great Admiral, a concert hall from a Great Musician.
RuinsTwo new outcomes in ancient ruins, defined for all eight difficulty levels.
Engine80 new mechanics and 45 changed code files — things the base game's code simply cannot do. The save format is unchanged.
InterfaceA quick-phrase wheel on the H key, the civilization's era bonus visible both when you pick it and on the loading screen, and other EUI fixes.
