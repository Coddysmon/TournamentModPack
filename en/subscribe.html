Subscription

Tiers, prices and donations. The mod pack is free — the subscription changes appearance only and unlocks nothing in the game itself.

The mod pack itself is free, and it will stay that way. The subscription unlocks nothing in the game: no unit, no building, no bonus, no access to matches. All it gives you is appearance: the choice of mouse cursor (every cursor in the set, from Bronze up), the font, colour, badge and styling of your name, the tier emblem in the launcher, a metal rim around the unit icon, and unit skins at Gold. Without a subscription you get the game's standard cursor; launcher themes and the profile avatar are free for everyone. A skin never changes a unit's stats: a player without a subscription loses nothing to a subscriber except the looks.

What the subscription gives you

Prices and the cursor line-up come from the same file the subscription service uses, so the site cannot show a price that does not exist.

Prices are in Russian roubles (RUB). Payment goes through a Russian payment provider inside the launcher; there is no card form on this site.

    How it works

    • You buy inside the launcher, section “Подписки” (Subscriptions). There is no payment button on this site and there never will be: a subscription is tied to your access code, and the code is issued by the launcher.
    • There is no sign-up. The code is the account. Copy it, enter it on another computer, and the subscription moves with you.
    • Paid cursors are not part of the common mod package. They are not sitting “locked” on everyone's disk — the launcher downloads them after payment. When a subscription ends, the launcher removes only those files; nothing of yours is lost.
    • We never see your payment details. You enter your card on the payment provider's side; no numbers and no tokens reach us, and nothing of the kind is stored anywhere here.
    • Auto-renewal is on by default. At checkout the “renew automatically” box is already ticked, and the next month is charged by itself. For a one-off 30 days, untick it before paying: such a purchase does not renew, and you extend it by buying again.
    • To cancel auto-renewal, write to nssmain@mail.ru from the e-mail you used at checkout, or quote your access code. We cancel by hand; access stays until the end of the period you paid for.
    • Paying means accepting the terms. What exactly you are buying, for how long, how to cancel and how refunds work is set out in the contract offer — it is a document under Russian law and exists in Russian only.
