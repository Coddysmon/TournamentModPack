What this is

Nova is a mod pack for Civilization V: its own civilizations, policy branches, resources, buildings and wonders, its own engine build and the EUI interface. It is installed not as an ordinary Workshop modification but as an expansion — the engine loads it by itself, you don't have to switch anything on in the mods screen, and multiplayer works.

The whole mod pack is free. There is no paid content in the game: the subscription only changes appearance — the mouse cursor, the styling of your name, the tier emblem and the metal rim around the unit icon, plus unit skins at the Gold tier. It has no effect whatsoever on balance or rules — more about the subscription.

An era bonus for every civilization

When you enter a new era you pick from four bonuses instead of three: the fourth belongs to your civilization alone. 43 national bonuses and 21 shared ones in total.

Three new policy branches

“Legacy” — 64 policies holding the era bonuses; “Wayfaring” — roads, caravans and keeping the empire connected; “Bulwark” — defence: walls, castle and arsenal stop being a pure drain on gold.

15 new luxuries

Beer, honey, cognac, cheese, rice, steel, platinum, sulphur, potato, barley, pineapple, limestone, caviar, feathers and red caviar — each with its own model on the tile and its own buildings.

41 new buildings and new wonders

Buildings that feed on the luxuries in the city; wonders with effects and icons of their own.

58 new beliefs

Pantheons tied to resources, and new tenets for religions.

A custom engine build

80 mechanics that simply do not exist in the base game's code. The save format is unchanged: old games still load.

The launcher

The program that gets the mod into the game and keeps it current. What it does:

  • installs and updates the mod, checking every file by hash — only what actually changed gets downloaded;
  • survives an interrupted update and wiped folders: the next start finishes the job instead of leaving the mod half-installed;
  • shows live Steam lobbies and the host's build tag — a version mismatch is visible before you join, not after a crash mid-match;
  • collects the game logs, hides the known noise and shows the first real error;
  • clears the game's database cache (it never touches saves or match history);
  • changes the cursor and the unit skins — both come with the subscription.
