Tournament rules

Registration and entry fee, qualification, how matches are played, play-offs and refereeing. You sign up through the launcher; signing up confirms that you have read these rules.

The tournament starts on 1 November. The entry fee is 600 roubles (RUB), paid through the launcher; the terms of the fee and of refunds are in section 10 of the contract offer (Russian only — it is a document under Russian law). You need Brave New World and Gods & Kings, an open Steam profile and Discord with working voice. Without at least one full stream you cannot reach the play-offs.

These rules are translated from the Russian original. If the two ever disagree, the Russian text is the one that counts — that is the version the referees work from. The tournament is run in Russian, and voice communication during matches is in Russian.

I. Registration

1. General

  1. The tournament starts on 1 November.
  2. If 30 participants have not signed up by 1 November, the tournament is declared void.

2. Eligibility

  1. An open Steam profile (visible to everyone).
  2. Ownership of Brave New World, Gods & Kings, the Ancient Wonders pack and all civilizations.
  3. A correctly installed launcher and patch.
  4. No name changes during the tournament (Steam, Twitch, Discord).
  5. A stable connection. If others lag because of you — replacement or disqualification.
  6. At registration the organiser may schedule a Discord interview: a check of common sense, knowledge of the game and working voice.
  7. The organiser may refuse an application without giving reasons.

3. What you have to do to take part

  1. Register through the launcher.
  2. Provide a link to your Twitch/YouTube — it is required at sign-up, without it the application is not accepted. You cannot reach the play-offs without streaming — at least one full stream is required. Provide your Discord as well.
  3. Wait for the organisers' approval.
  4. Pay the entry fee — 600 roubles (about 7.12 USD). Payment goes through the launcher: the “Турниры” (Tournaments) tab → “Оплатить взнос” (Pay the fee). The launcher opens the payment page in your browser, the receipt arrives at the e-mail you give at checkout, and the paid mark appears next to your name in the participant list within a few minutes. If the button is missing, update the launcher. The fee goes towards running the tournament and the prize fund; refund terms are in section 10 of the contract offer.

4. General duties of players

4.1. Fair play

  • Forbidden: cheats, duping (turn reloading, deliberate lag), debug mode, tampering with saves, watching other participants' streams or recordings before the match ends, receiving information from outsiders (the common chat only).
  • Allowed: ignoring the zone of control when capturing a civilian unit, spying to see what a city is building, firing from a satellite city to reveal what it is building.

4.2. Responsibilities in matches

  • At least 3 games in qualification.
  • Take the initiative when a match has to be resumed, and report problems in good time.
  • In the play-offs — stay reachable and agree on times.
  • Play to win to the very end. Violations include: disbanding your army, offering no resistance when your cities are taken, nuking yourself without reason.
  • Do not be late, and warn people when plans change.

4.3. Discord

  • Install Discord in advance, set voice to push-to-talk, and join the official server.
  • During a match you must be in the voice channel with the other players.

4.4. Politics

  • Discussing political topics is forbidden in tournament chats and on streams.
  • This covers: what the streamer says, the audio track, images and text on screen, the channel description, viewer and bot messages in chat, and the stream title.

II. Qualification round

1. Dates

  • Qualification runs up to and including 22 November.
  • Resumed sessions are not scheduled after that date.
  • Matches started on the last day are played without resumption.

2. Format

  • Each participant plays no more than 5 games.
  • A win is 1 point, a loss 0. Points may be deducted for breaking the rules.
  • The final ranking is by total points.
  • Ties are broken by the Buchholz score.

The Buchholz score

The sum of all points your opponents scored over the tournament. The stronger your opponents were, the higher your score.

Example: two players have 5 points each, but the first one's opponents scored 18 points in total and the second one's scored 9. The first player is placed higher.

The truncated Buchholz score

If two players match on both points and full Buchholz score, truncation is applied:

  1. The result of the weakest opponent (the one with the fewest points) is subtracted from the opponents' total.
  2. If the scores are still equal, the result of the next weakest opponent is subtracted, and so on.
  3. The player with the higher “cleaned” score wins the tie.

Example: Player A and Player B both have 4 points and the same full Buchholz score (20). A's weakest opponent scored 1 point, B's scored 2. After truncation: A — 19, B — 18. Player A wins the tie.

3. Unique opponents

  • The first two matches must be against opponents you have not met yet.
  • You may play the same player no more than twice.
  • You may not have more than two unfinished matches at once.

4. Match deadlines

  • A match must be finished within 4 days of starting.
  • If you need longer — warn the organisers in advance.

  • If there is no stream recording of a winning match, the penalty is 100 % of the points scored in that match.

III. How matches are played

1. Private messages

  • Forbidden.

2. Actions at the end of the timer and at the start

  • Shift-turns are forbidden.
  • Moves in the last seconds are forbidden if they lead to double turns or leave the opponent no time to answer (including a massed attack or move at the end of a turn). The opponent may roll the timer back. If this is abused, a referee may order part of your units to be disbanded.
    • Note: after a timer rollback, dropping paratroopers and setting aircraft to intercept are matters of agreement. A timely rollback avoids having to load a save. Do not abuse it.
  • Forbidden to take an action and end your turn when the other participants have already ended theirs.
  • Forbidden to roll the timer back or pause without an agreed reason.
  • There are no limits on upgrading or buying units, except spaceship parts and buildings in a city — opponents must have a chance to respond to those.
  • Allowed at any moment: changing or setting what a city produces, and using Great Engineers.

3. Limits on military action

  • After a timer rollback it is forbidden to repeat a landing (paratroopers, XCOM) or to set aircraft to intercept again.
    • Exception: if a player rejoined the lobby — by agreement.
  • Repeated, agreed pillaging of caravans is forbidden (one player builds them, the other pillages, the gold is shared).
    • Not to be confused with: pillaging a single caravan going to a city-state for a quest, or compensating for caravans — that is allowed.

4. Relations with city-states

  • Once peace is signed you may do anything you like with city-states, including those allied to your opponent.
  • If you have agreed that a city-state is off limits, you may not fight it, pillage it, farm promotions on it, build up a Great General on it or eat away its territory. The agreement is binding for as long as you physically cannot declare war on that player (a non-aggression pact is active, or one turn has passed since peace).

5. Verbal agreements, defensive pacts, peace treaties, the World Congress

  • A non-aggression pact is made through the game's interface.
  • Verbal agreements have no standing, except for:
    • an agreement that city-states are off limits;
    • an agreement about placing citadels.
  • Forbidden after peace and while a non-aggression pact is active:
    • blocking tiles to prevent movement or the capture of cities;
      • Exception: if the opponent is using the peace to prepare an attack, you may get in the way.
    • blocking a nuclear strike;
    • eating away the opponent's territory with Great Generals (unless agreed otherwise).
    All of these apply for as long as you physically cannot declare war.
  • If, when making peace, you agreed to hand over a Great General and/or to place or remove citadels, do it as soon as possible.
  • Forbidden, when you are losing, to give another player cities, great people, luxuries, strategic resources, gold and the like for free or nearly free.
    • Exception: repaying purely monetary debts (without converting great people, luxuries and so on into gold).
    • This does not cover gifts of military units and workers, with one caveat: not if you could still have resisted with them.

6. What to do about rule violations and reloads

If the violation cannot be established without a referee

  1. The player contacts any referee and tells the others in the match.
  2. Action against opponents stops, the timer is paused.
  3. The referee has the final word.

If the violation can be established without a referee

  1. Those affected propose ways to carry on.
  2. If everyone agrees — the match continues.
    • Participants may refuse without giving reasons.
  3. If there is no agreement — follow point VII.1.
  • Forbidden to carry on playing if a player has dropped out of the lobby. Make a save and pause.
  • Forbidden to take any action against other players until they are back in.
  • A player may demand that actions be repeated after a disconnect, a desync or a reload:
    • capturing or recapturing a city;
    • pillaging a strategic or rare resource, or a road;
    • chopping forest or jungle, draining a marsh;
    • repairing a resource or road, or occupying the tile they are on;
    • repairing, placing or pillaging a citadel;
    • killing or capturing great people.

Important. Every disputed situation involving a desync or a reload is decided by the referees case by case.

IV. Organising matches (in brief)

1. When matches are played

  • Default playing time: 19:00–02:00 Moscow time.
  • Any other time can be agreed.
  • After 4 hours in one session you may ask for a break (shared, up to 30 minutes).
  • After 6 hours you may ask for a save and a resumed session (warn people in advance).

2. Creating an event in qualification

  • The event is created in the launcher: start time, and the time of the save if one is needed.
  • You play with the first five who sign up. Exceptions:
    • the ones who signed up are not tournament participants;
    • the rules on repeat opponents have to be respected (priority goes to whoever signed up earlier).
  • Once the line-up is settled — create a room in Discord and talk through the details.
  • Before the bans all 6 players join the lobby to check the connection (a few turns).
  • The host is the organiser by default. Everyone turns on autosave every turn.
  • The organiser marks the start or the resumption in the launcher.

3. Joining an event in qualification

  • Find the event in the launcher.
  • Check whether anyone there is someone you have already played (the repeat-opponent rules).
  • Sign up, add the organiser as a friend, wait for the Discord invite.
  • If there is a save, take the initiative yourself instead of waiting for the host.
  • You may be signed up for no more than 2 events (tell them about each other).
  • You may not have more than 2 unfinished matches (matches must finish within 4 days).

4. Play-offs

  • Participants and slots are known in advance. The player in the first slot is the organiser.
  • The organiser notes who has taken a restart (a non-technical one).

5. Choosing civilizations

  • Civilizations are picked through the in-game interface.
  • Once chosen, a civilization cannot be changed (including after a restart).
  • Disputes go to the referees.
  • Swapping the sets of civilizations you were dealt is forbidden.

6. Restarts and annulled matches

  • A match may be annulled for technical reasons only before turn 100. First try a save, a change of host, a replacement player. The decision is made after asking the tournament organiser.
  • The right to a restart:
    • In qualification — nobody has it.
    • In the play-offs: the top 9 get 1 restart, the top 3 get 2.
    • Announce the restart (RE) between turns 0 and 10, by private message to a player without a restart (or to a referee).
    • Announcing before turn 10 or changing your mind is forbidden.
    • If a restart is still in hand at the start of turn 11, it is carried out.
    • The organiser posts about it in the tournament thread.
  • Automatic restart:
    • If capitals are 7 tiles apart or less, an automatic restart may be claimed.
    • It applies during the first 20 turns.
    • It does not apply if both players agree to carry on.
    • In the play-offs it may not be discussed during the first 10 turns.
    • Whoever notices it must say so (not before turn 11 in the play-offs).
    • A capital may only be founded within 5 tiles of the starting settler.
    • Abusing this (wandering around with the settler to find a capital) is forbidden.
    • If the settler is lost to barbarians, there is no restart.
  • Replacing a player: the referee committee decides (postpone, save, carry on with the AI or with a disinterested person).

7. Game settings

  • Forbidden mods: the one showing which units have moved, and the multi-click aircraft mod.
  • All mods affecting the integrity of the game must be removed.

8. Voice communication in Discord

  • Under a congress embargo — full mute in Discord until the embargo is lifted (1 minute to comply).
  • Breaking this: −0.5 points; ignoring it: disqualification.

9. Leaving a match

  • In qualification you may leave at any time.
  • You may leave within 10 turns of losing or recapturing your capital.
  • In the play-offs, once at least one player has left for a new time slot, a player may be let out by an IRR vote.
  • When someone leaves, the host must kick them immediately.

VI. Play-off rules

1. Dates

  1. The whole play-off stage lasts 2 weeks.
  2. Matches #1.1, #1.2, #1.3 — up to and including 6 December.
  3. Matches #2.1, #2.2, #2.3 — up to and including 13 December.
  4. The final #3.1 — one day, no resumption, at a time the players agree, up to and including 20 December.

2. Overall format

  1. The tournament is played as FFA6 (6 players).
  2. Each match has exactly one winner. The winner gets 1 point, everyone else 0.

3. Who reaches the play-offs

Step 1. Automatic qualification

Everyone who scored 3, 4 or 5 points in qualification goes through automatically (unless 18 people end up on 3 points).

21 people go through in total. Places 19, 20 and 21 are reserves (for replacements in matches #2.1–2.3), and so on down the table.

Step 2. Filling up by Buchholz

If Step 1 leaves fewer players than there are places, players on 2 points are added in descending order of Buchholz score (the sum of all their opponents' points over the 5 rounds).

Step 3. Seeding into the first-round matches

The winners of matches #1.1, #1.2 and #1.3 go straight to the final.

The seeding is done by the tournament organiser, live on stream.

Step 6. Replacements and second-round matches

  • If the winners of #1.1–1.3 or ordinary players cannot play in the final or in the play-offs, replacements are taken starting from place 19.

Step 7. The final

  • The final is 6 people.
  • Line-up: the winners of matches #1.1–1.3 and #2.1–2.3.
  • Format — FFA6, one match.
  • The winner is the TOURNAMENT CHAMPION.
  • The other finalists take places 2 to 6 according to the result of the final.

4. Replacements

  1. The candidate list is made up of players not taking part in that round, ordered by their qualification places.
  2. In the first round replacements start from place 19. The replaced player goes back onto the candidate list.
  3. If a stand-in reaches the final, they play in the final, even if the player they replaced has come back. If they do not reach it, they return to the candidate list.
  4. The second round works the same way, from the candidate list.
  5. Disputes are settled by the referee committee:
    • a player has not responded for 2 days;
    • a player is not taking part in choosing a time;
    • a player has vanished or cannot play;
    • players cannot agree on a time — the one with the least availability is replaced (a last resort).
  6. If a player is absent at the start:
    • the organiser contacts the candidates in order of priority;
    • waits 10 minutes for an answer;
    • no answer — the next candidate;
    • once chosen, the replacement is agreed with the referee committee and then made.

5. Streaming the play-offs

  1. A stream is mandatory for every play-off match.
  2. The stream must be saved and available afterwards.
  3. Emote-only mode is switched on (on Twitch: /emoteonly and /emoteonlyoff).

6. Final standings

  1. The tournament winner is the winner of match #3.1 (the final).
  2. The other finalists take places 2 to 6 according to the result of the final.
  3. The players in the first slots take on the duties of event organisers.
  4. In every match, places are determined the same way as in qualification. If the averages are equal, priority goes to whoever finished higher in qualification.
  5. Each play-off round gets exactly one week. Every player must post in the event within 2 days to avoid being replaced.

VII. Organisers and refereeing

1. Organisers and the referee committee

  • JAGUARRR, CODDYS — the tournament organisers.
  • They are responsible for running it, the format and the organisation.
  • They rule on clear-cut cases.

2. Penalties

For any breach of the rules discovered during a match:

  • A deduction from accumulated points (qualification only).
  • A verbal warning (УП).
    • Two verbal warnings = a yellow card.
  • A yellow card (ЖК).
    • Two yellow cards = a red card — disqualification.
  • A red card (КК) — disqualification.
  • Being made to repeat the actions taken before the violation (after a reload).
    • Exception: a city-state coup, and “convenient” damage in attack or defence — those cannot be repeated.
  • Being made to take actions that make up for the violation (at the referee's discretion).

Complaints about violations and about a match result must be made within 24 hours of the end of the match being reported.
